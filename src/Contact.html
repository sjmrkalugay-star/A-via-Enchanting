Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place a custom order?

Simply fill out the contact form above with your requirements, or reach out to us via phone, email, or social media. We'll discuss your vision, color preferences, and any special requests to create the perfect bouquet for you.

What areas do you deliver to?

We offer free delivery within General Santos City and surrounding areas. For deliveries outside our regular service area, please contact us for shipping arrangements and rates.

How long does it take to make a custom bouquet?

Most custom orders take 3-5 business days to complete, depending on complexity and current order volume. Rush orders may be accommodated for an additional fee—contact us to discuss your timeline.

Can I see samples before ordering?

Absolutely! Visit our shop during business hours to see our portfolio and current displays. You can also browse our Instagram and Facebook pages for recent creations and customer photos.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept GCash, bank transfers, and cash payments. For online orders, payment confirmation is required before we begin processing your custom bouquet.

Do you offer gift wrapping?

Yes! All our bouquets come beautifully wrapped and ready to gift at no additional charge. We can also include personalized cards upon request.
